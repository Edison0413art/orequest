Who we are

Bringing Zimbabwe's lithium to a world that's electrifying.

Lithium is the metal of the energy transition — and Zimbabwe holds some of the richest hard-rock lithium on Earth. Orequest Private Limited was founded to turn that geological advantage into reliable, battery-grade supply for a decarbonising world.

We pair our own mining claims with exclusive sales agreements held with mine owners across the Bikita, Goromonzi, Mutoko, Kamativi and Mberengwa districts — consolidating ore that would otherwise reach the market in fragments into one clean, traceable chain of custody.

  • Owned claimsTitle-held lithium ground in proven spodumene–petalite pegmatites.
  • Exclusive offtake from regional ownersSole-purchase agreements with claim owners across our five districts.
  • University of Zimbabwe partnershipJoint lithium-processing research and graduate training to build local technical skill.
  • Mine-to-port controlOne chain of custody from blast to bagged, assayed export concentrate.

Backed by national policy

Aligned with Zimbabwe's lithium ambition.

Government has made lithium and local value-addition a national priority — and Orequest operates squarely within that framework.

01

Government encouragement

Zimbabwe actively promotes investment in lithium extraction and beneficiation as a pillar of its critical-minerals strategy, with supportive licensing for compliant producers.

02

Local value-addition

National policy favours in-country processing. Our on-site beneficiation supports the value-addition agenda rather than exporting raw ore alone.

03

Lawful title & export

We operate on duly registered mining title and export through approved, documented channels with independent assay on every consignment.

04

Investment incentives

We work within Zimbabwe's fiscal and special-economic incentives designed to attract responsible mining capital and create local jobs.
